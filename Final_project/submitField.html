What is This?

This is a tool to determine field locations and size. Field locations must be identified to properly perfrom analysis.

Where to Start:
  • Select the pentagon tool on the left side of the screen.
  • Click around the outside of your field to draw the boundary.
  • Select finish, then give your field a name.
  • Your field will download as a GeoJSON ready to submit.
