What is NDVI?
  • Plant "greenness" index
  • Range from -1.0 through +1.0
  • Uses invisible Near Infrared spectra
  • Can detect plant injury before we can see it
  • Helps determining areas of concern
Why Soil pH?
  • Nutrient availablility depends on pH
  • Ideally 6.0-6.8 slightly acidic
  • Pesticide efficiency
